Why this rating

  • Width in millimetres needs a reference in the photo.

CrackWatch is a photo aid, not a structural inspection. It does not replace a qualified structural engineer. Lighting, focus, angle and the reference all change the numbers.

Turn pixels into millimetres

Put a coin flat on the same surface as the crack, or pick two points you already know. The camera should be square to the wall. A ₹10 coin is 27 mm across.

Has it grown? Beta

Upload an older photo and a newer one of the same crack. CrackWatch lines them up with ORB features and a homography, then looks for crack that is in the new photo and not the old one. This step is in beta: the first comparison downloads OpenCV (about 10 MB) and stops after 10 seconds if it does not start. The photo check above does not need it.

How the rating is decided

    The outline is a crack-shaped filter, not a chat model: a multi-scale black-hat to lift thin dark lines, and a Frangi ridge filter to ignore round texture. Length is the centreline. Width is the dark dip across that line. The rating uses the widest point, plus the pattern.

    Runs entirely in your browser. No photo is uploaded. Not a substitute for a structural engineer.
